# Name availability and legal/registration landscape for a Syria-only classifieds app (as of 9 Oct 2026)

> **Method and verification limits (read first).** Direct lookups were **blocked** in this research environment. curl to `rdap.org`, `rdap.verisign.com`, `play.google.com` and `itunes.apple.com` was refused by the egress proxy ("connect_rejected", organization policy), and WebFetch could not resolve any host (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND` for apps.apple.com, play.google.com, rdap.org, en.wikipedia.org). As a result:
> - **No domain was checked through RDAP/whois.** Domain status below is "not verified" unless a web page showed the domain in use.
> - **Google Play and APKPure could not be searched directly.** App conflicts come from search-indexed **Apple App Store pages** (apps.apple.com), App Store tracker pages (mwm.ai, appfigures and similar) and a few Android download sites. An app that is only on Google Play and is not indexed by the search engine would be missed.
> - **Social handles (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X) could not be checked at all.**
> - **The WIPO Global Brand Database, TMview/DPMA and TÜRKPATENT could not be queried.** Trademark conflicts come only from web search.
> - "Cited" findings below come from search-engine summaries of the linked pages. I did not open the pages directly.
>
> **Rating scale used.** **Clear**: no app or company with this name found in marketplace/shopping or in Syria. **Partially taken**: the exact or a near-identical name is used, but in another category or region. **Crowded**: several users of the name, including marketplace/shopping apps in the Arab world. **Blocked**: the exact name is already used by a classifieds/marketplace product in Syria or by a dominant regional classifieds brand, or the name is too generic to protect.

## Q1. Conflict/availability check for the 20 seed candidate names

### Takeaway
Four seed names are effectively **Blocked**: **Laqta/لقطة** and **Bazar Sham/بازار الشام** are already Syrian marketplace apps, **Mustamal** collides with Saudi classifieds giant Mstaml, and **Sooq Syria/سوق سوريا** is generic and already used as a tagline. **Souqna, Basta, Dukkan, Fursa and Khan** are **Crowded** with Arab-world shopping or classifieds apps. The cleanest seed names found are **Mazboot/مزبوط, Shatara/شطارة, Jaarak/جارك, Harati/حارتي, Baddak/بدّك, Halla2/هلّق and Bala Wasta/بلا واسطة**, which turned up no same-name app (search-level check only).

### Inferences
- **Domains (not verified; inference only):** short dictionary or name words (basta, khan, dukkan, fursa, yasmina, dallal, souqna) are very likely already registered in .com, so expect to buy one on the aftermarket or add a suffix (e.g. `getX.com`, `Xapp.com`, `X.sy`). The coined or dialect forms (mazboot, shatara, jaarak, baddak, halla2, balawasta, hawwesh) are more likely to be free. RDAP checks are needed before deciding. The one domain confirmed in use is **basta.app**, from the contact email in the Shopify listing.
- **Arabic and transliteration issues (my linguistic assessment, not sourced):**
  - Names that use Arabizi numerals (**Bi3ha, Halla2, Lu2ta**) are hard to find in app stores and hard to say aloud in ads. Play/App Store search does not reliably match "3" to "a" or "2" to the hamza.
  - **Mazboot/Mazbout/Mazbut** and **Shatara/Shatarah** have several plausible spellings. If chosen, the app should claim all of them as handles and as keywords.
  - **Mazboot (مزبوط)**: in Levantine it means "correct / exactly right / OK", and in coffee "medium-sweet". Both are positive, with no negative meaning found in EN/DE/FR/TR.
  - **Basta**: in German "und basta!" means "and that's final!", which is harmless but slightly curt.
  - **Dukkan**: in Turkish "dükkân" means "shop", which is neutral to positive.
  - **Khanna**: a common Indian surname, so it reads as a person's name to diaspora audiences.
  - **Baddak**: begins with English "bad", a mild negative for English speakers.
  - **Fursa**: close to German "Furz" ("fart"), a weak risk.
  - None of the 20 names had an obviously embarrassing meaning in English, German, French or Turkish, but this assessment was not checked against native-speaker sources.
- **Strategic reading:** the "Souq/Sooq" root is saturated (OpenSooq, Sooqz, Souq Tijari, Souq.com legacy, SOOQNA, Safakat's "سوق سوريا" tagline). Any Souq-based name will be hard to distinguish in search and will probably be refused as descriptive for marketplace services (see Q4). Dialect words that carry a feeling rather than a category (مزبوط, شطارة, بدّك, هلّق, جارك, حارتي) are more distinctive and appear free.
- "Harati/حارتي" and "Jaarak/جارك" fit a neighbourhood/local-trust positioning, but "Hartna/حارتنا" already exists as a neighbourhood network with ads, so Harati is one letter away from it.

### Gaps
- Google Play and APKPure were not searched directly (proxy blocked). Android-only apps with these names may exist undetected.
- No RDAP/whois results for any .com, .app, .sy or .com.sy domain. All domain status is unverified.
- Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and X handles were not checked.
- WIPO Global Brand Database, TMview (EUIPO/DPMA) and TÜRKPATENT were not queried, so registered trademarks such as "BASTA" (likely numerous in the EU) and "DUKKAN"/"DÜKKAN" (likely in Turkey) are unconfirmed.
- The Syrian DCIP trademark register is not searchable online from here. Local Syrian marks with these names are unknown.
- No source confirmed whether OpenSooq currently operates a Syria site.

## Q3. .sy domain registration: operator, rules, cost and practicality (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
.sy is run by Syria's **National Agency for Network Services (NANS / الهيئة الوطنية لخدمات الشبكة)**, a government body. Since the mid-2025 US sanctions relief, international registrars market .sy to foreigners at roughly **US$200/year**, but sources still disagree on whether local presence or a local agent with legalized documents is required. A Syrian-registered company (which the founder will need anyway) avoids that issue.

### Cited Findings
- IANA redelegated .SY to the National Agency for Network Services in 2011. The registry is a Syrian government entity and is to be operated in-country — [IANA redelegation report, Jan 2011](https://www.iana.org/reports/2011/sy-report-07jan2011.html)
- NANS was created under the Digital Signature and Network Services law. It manages Syria's TLDs (.SY and any Arabic IDN ccTLD) and sets registration policy. Private companies can register .sy names, and government bodies were moving their sites to .sy (Enab Baladi, 2023) — [Enab Baladi](https://www.enabbaladi.net/667979/تجاريًا-ما-فوائد-شراء-نطاق-إلكتروني-مع/)
- June 2025: "the recent lifting of U.S. sanctions has paved the way for businesses and individuals worldwide to register .SY domain names" (TAG-Domains/AGIP press release; requirements and pricing on request) — [AGIP news](https://www.agip.com/News.aspx?id=22335)
- **Conflicting statements on local presence:**
  - "Registrants need a local presence in Syria or be a Syrian entity" — [domaindetails.com](https://domaindetails.com/tlds/sy)
  - Since 2009, foreigners can register only through a local representative agent, a manual process needing an application form, a notarized and consulate-legalized request, a notarized power of attorney and certified business-registration documents — [letsdomains.com](https://letsdomains.com/domain-database/sy)
  - A price aggregator lists "local presence not required", with AtakDomain cheapest at **US$199 first year / US$219 renewal** (prices updated 29 Jul 2026) — [domainoffer.net .sy](https://domainoffer.net/price-compare-sy)
  - Gandi offers .sy only to Corporate Services contract customers, at ¥173,325/yr renewal (Japan pricing) — [Gandi](https://www.gandi.net/ja/domain/tld/sy)
- A price-comparison page exists for **.com.sy** (updated 13 Sep 2026); I did not see its contents — [domainoffer.net .com.sy](https://domainoffer.net/price-compare-com.sy)
- Example of a Syrian platform on .sy: Forsa.sy (jobs) — [Qureos](https://www.qureos.com/hiring-guide/top-recruitment-platforms-in-syria)

### Inferences
- For a Syria-only, Arabic-first brand, **.sy is a credible trust signal and is now technically obtainable**, but at ~US$200/yr through foreign resellers it costs about 10–20x a .com. Registering directly with NANS through a local entity is probably cheaper; I found no fee schedule.
- Most Syrian marketplace apps found so far are identified by their app-store listing rather than a website, so a .com or .app is not mandatory at launch. Securing **X.com (or a close variant) plus X.sy** is a sensible defensive pair, and **.app** is cheap insurance.
- Because the app is Syria-only and the founder will need a Syrian legal entity (for trademarks, payments and Meta/Google ads accounts), the local-presence ambiguity largely disappears once that entity exists.

### Gaps
- No official NANS policy page or fee schedule was reachable (direct fetch was blocked and searches returned none). Current NANS fees for a local registrant are unknown.
- I could not confirm whether the 2025 sanctions relief changed the local-agent/legalization requirement. Sources conflict.
- No reliable data on which TLD Syrian startups typically use.

## Q4. Trademark registration in Syria

### Takeaway
Trademarks are filed with the **Directorate of Commercial and Industrial Property Protection (DCIP), dcip.gov.sy**, which WIPO lists under the Ministry of Economy and Industry, under **Law No. 8 of 2007**. Syria is a **first-to-file** jurisdiction and has been a **Madrid Protocol** member since **5 Aug 2004** (confirmed). Expect about **8–12 months** to registration, a 90-day opposition window and 10-year terms. Arabic is effectively mandatory: Latin marks need an Arabic translation or transliteration, and marks used on Syrian goods must show Arabic at equal size.

### Cited Findings
- **Authority:** WIPO's country profile lists the Directorate of Industrial and Commercial Property Protection (DCIP) under the **Ministry of Economy and Industry**, with website dcip.gov.sy. Madrid contact: intreg@dcip.gov.sy — [WIPO country profile Syria](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/country-profiles/sy); [WIPO contact](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/country-profiles/contact?code=SY)
- In 2020, WIPO described the directorate as part of the Ministry of Internal Trade and Consumer Protection, reopened to the public on 1 June 2020. The ministry placement has changed over time — [WIPO Madrid notice 2020/44](https://wipo.int/edocs/madrdocs/en/2020/madrid_2020_44.pdf)
- **Law:** Law No. 8 of 2007 on Trademarks, Geographical Indications, Industrial Drawings and Designs entered into force 12 Apr 2007 — [WIPO Lex](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/10781). It "remains the primary legislation in force" per Saba IP (INTA Annual Meeting 2026) — [INTA/Saba IP 2026](https://inta.foleon.com/am-2026/am26-day3/why-trademark-protection-cannot-wait-in-syria)
- A draft new Syrian IP law (including cancellation after 3 years of non-use) was reported under consideration. Its enactment is unconfirmed — [World Trademark Review](https://www.worldtrademarkreview.com/article/new-syrian-intellectual-property-law-under-consideration)
- **Arabic requirement:**
  - Foreign-language marks may be filed, but Arabic translations or transliterations are required — [INTA/Saba IP 2026](https://inta.foleon.com/am-2026/am26-day3/why-trademark-protection-cannot-wait-in-syria)
  - The executive regulations (Art. 5, per search snippet) require marks on goods of national origin produced and circulated in Syria to be written in Arabic, with foreign letters allowed alongside at equal size. Marks only in foreign letters are allowed for export-only goods and for imported goods — [WIPO Lex text 446211](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/446211)
- **Madrid:**
  - Syria deposited its accession to both the Madrid Agreement and the Protocol on 5 May 2004. In force for Syria **5 Aug 2004** (Decree No. 92 of 26 Mar 2004) — [WIPO Information Notice 13/2004](https://wipo.int/documents/d/madrid-system/information-notices-en-2004-madrid_2004_13.pdf); [AGIP](https://www.agip.com/news/en/news/314)
  - Syria's Madrid individual fee rose from **CHF 134 to CHF 215 per class** from 18 Sep 2025 — [WIPO Information Notice 10/2025](https://www.wipo.int/documents/d/madrid-system/information-notices-en-2025-madrid_2025_10_e.docx)
- **Fees:** Ministerial Decision 1448/2025 raised trademark and design publication fees, some up to 50x and others about 10x. It applies to new and pending applications with unpaid publication fees (newsletter dated Apr 2025, drawing on AGIP) — [Country Index](https://country-index.com/country_surveys.aspx?ID=91); history of earlier increases — [AGIP](https://www.agip.com/news/en/news/19196)
- **Timeline and term:**
  - Registration takes **8–10 months** per one AGIP page and **10–12 months** per another; a search takes 15 days. Documents can be completed within 6 months after filing, with fines — [AGIP country service](https://www.agip.com/Agip-Country-Service/en/160/T/2)
  - Opposition period is **90 days** from publication, the term is **10 years from the application date**, and there is a **3-year use requirement** (page updated 25 Apr 2026) — [Country Index](https://country-index.com/country_surveys.aspx?ID=91)
  - Examination covers formalities plus absolute and relative grounds — [Country Index](https://country-index.com/country_surveys.aspx?ID=91)
  - The Office may require amendments to avoid confusion with earlier rights, within 3 months — [AGIP (DE)](https://www.agip.com/Agip-Country-Service/de/160/t)
- **First-to-file:** exclusive ownership goes to the first party to complete formal registration, and squatting risk rises during economic transitions — [Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/the-critical-importance-of-securing-trademark-rights-in-syria-following-the-lifting-of-trade-restrictions/)
- Professional fees seen online, e.g. US$999/class plus government fee for a renewal service, are agent fees, not official costs — [TrademarkElite](https://www.trademarkelite.com/trademark/trademark-renew/SY)

### Inferences
- **File the Arabic and Latin forms together as a composite mark** (e.g. "مزبوط MAZBOOT" with logo) in **Class 35** (online marketplace / advertising services), **Class 9** (downloadable app) and **Class 42** (SaaS/platform). Filing both scripts covers the Arabic-display rule and the transliteration requirement.
- Because Syria is first-to-file and has had a post-sanctions influx of foreign filings, file **before** public launch or marketing. Run a DCIP clearance search first (about 15 days per AGIP).
- **Generic words:** I found no Syrian-specific source on descriptiveness. Under standard international practice (Paris Convention Art. 6quinquies, which Syria follows as a Paris Union member), سوق ("market"), مستعمل ("used"), بيع ("sale") or "سوق سوريا" for marketplace services would likely be refused as descriptive or generic on absolute grounds, or registered only as a stylized logo with weak protection. **This is an inference, not a verified Syrian rule.**
- A Madrid filing from a foreign base designating Syria is possible (CHF 215/class individual fee), but a Syria-only business will more simply file nationally through a local agent.

### Gaps
- Official DCIP fee schedule and the actual amounts after Decision 1448/2025: not found.
- Text of Law 8/2007 on descriptive/generic marks: not retrieved. How DCIP treats generic Arabic words such as سوق is unconfirmed.
- No DCIP online search tool was accessible. Whether the WIPO Global Brand Database includes Syrian national marks is unconfirmed.
- I could not confirm whether DCIP's ministry has changed again in 2025–2026 (the Ministry of Economy and Industry is per WIPO's undated profile).

## Q5. App-store availability for Syria (users and developers) after the 2025–2026 sanctions easing, and what it means for name discoverability

### Takeaway
**Users:**
- **Apple:** the App Store was restored in Syria in **Sept 2025**.
- **Google:** Syria's minister announced Google Play access **without VPN on 17 Jan 2026**, after Google removed Syria from its restricted list (Ads) on **13 Aug 2025**.
- **US sanctions:** the US removed Syria from the State Sponsors of Terrorism list on **24 Aug 2026**, the last major US barrier.

**Developers:** I found **no official confirmation** that Syria-based developers can open Play Console merchant accounts or enrol in the Apple Developer Program. Card and payment access is still a practical blocker, so publishing through an entity or account outside Syria, or sideloading APKs, may still be needed in practice.

### Cited Findings
- **US legal context:**
  - OFAC General License 25 (23 May 2025) effectively allowed tech companies to serve Syrians — [EFF](https://kittens.eff.org/node/110796)
  - OFAC lifted Syria sanctions in July 2025, and BIS created the "Syria Peace and Prosperity" licence exception on 2 Sep 2025 (per Apple's trade-compliance page) — [Apple Trade Compliance](https://www.apple.com/nl/legal/more-resources/gtc.html)
  - The Caesar Act was repealed in December 2025, though not all US Syria-related sanctions were automatically lifted — [Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/the-critical-importance-of-securing-trademark-rights-in-syria-following-the-lifting-of-trade-restrictions/)

Remaining SST countries: Cuba, Iran, North Korea — [Al Jazeera, 24 Aug 2026](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/24/us-removes-syria-from-state-sponsor-of-terrorism-list); [Katten](https://quickreads.ext.katten.com/post/102nrii/the-united-states-removes-syria-from-the-state-sponsors-of-terrorism-list)

### Inferences
- **For a free classifieds app (no paid download), the user side is now workable on both stores.** The free Android-first launch on Google Play is viable as of 2026. Paid features such as featured listings would need Play Billing (merchant-account eligibility unconfirmed) or off-store payment where policy allows.
- **Developer account:** until Syrian merchant support is confirmed, the safest route is registering the Play Console and Apple Developer accounts to an entity with a supported card and bank, e.g. a founder's company in Germany or Turkey. The app's store **seller name** will then show that entity, which matters for local trust and for consistency with the Syrian trademark owner.
- **Discoverability implications for the name:**
  1. Many Syrian users still install via APK links shared on WhatsApp/Telegram/Facebook (inference from years of Play blocking). The name must be **easy to say and spell in both scripts**, with no numerals.
  2. Store search in Syria will be largely **Arabic-script**, so the Arabic form must be distinctive. Generic سوق/مستعمل/بيع names will be buried under OpenSooq, Haraj, Safakat and others.
  3. Put the Arabic name in the store title (e.g. "مزبوط - بيع وشراء المستعمل") so both scripts are indexed.
  4. Publish a signed APK on the brand's own .sy/.com domain, and possibly on Huawei AppGallery and APKPure, under **exactly the same name and package ID**, to deter clones. APK mirror sites will host copies regardless.
