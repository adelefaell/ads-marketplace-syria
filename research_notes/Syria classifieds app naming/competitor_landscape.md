# Competitive landscape: classifieds and second-hand marketplace apps serving Syria (as of October 2026)

> Method note (read first): WebFetch could not reach any domain in this environment (DNS failure for play.google.com, apps.apple.com, enabbaladi.net, unblocksyria.com and others). Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine result pages and their snippets/summaries of the cited URLs, not from a direct read of each page. Store figures (downloads, ratings, update dates) are as shown in those snippets and may be a few weeks or months stale. Logo and colour cues could not be checked visually for almost any competitor (see Gaps). Research date: 9 Oct 2026.

## Q1. Which regional/global classifieds players actually serve Syria today?

### Takeaway
OpenSooq (السوق المفتوح) is the only large regional horizontal with a live Syria site (sy.opensooq.com) and a very large Syrian Facebook audience. Dubizzle/OLX, Haraj, 4Sale, Bezaat and Mstaml show no evidence of a Syria product. Facebook Marketplace is reported blocked or unreliable, so Syrians use Facebook groups instead.

### Cited Findings
**OpenSooq / السوق المفتوح ("The Open Market")**
- Has a dedicated Syria site at sy.opensooq.com (Arabic title: "السوق المفتوح : سوق سوريا المفتوح : سيارات : عقارات : بيوت : للبيع : خدمات : وظائف"). It lists cars, apartments, real estate and jobs, shows prices in SYP (for example SYP 2,000,000 on a sample listing) and covers Damascus, Aleppo, Homs and other cities — [sy.opensooq.com/ar](https://sy.opensooq.com/ar); [OpenSooq Syria real estate](https://sy.opensooq.com/ar/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA); [Damascus apartments](https://sy.opensooq.com/ar/%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%B4%D9%82/%D8%B9%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AA/%D8%B4%D9%82%D9%82-%D9%84%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B9)
- Its About page positions it as "منصة الإعلانات المبوبة الأولى لبيع وشراء العقارات والسيارات والإلكترونيات وأكثر بدون عمولة" ("the No. 1 classifieds platform... without commission"). It launched across the region in 2013 and says it operates in 20 countries — [sy.opensooq.com about-us](https://sy.opensooq.com/ar/site/about-us); [opensooq.com](https://www.opensooq.com/en)
- English Syria site title: "Syria Classifieds: Cars, Property, Jobs & More". It also has a "Ready to Sell in Syria? List Anything with Us" sell page — [sy.opensooq.com/en](https://sy.opensooq.com/en); [sell-any-thing](https://sy.opensooq.com/en/sell-any-thing)
- The Facebook page @OpenSooq.Syria shows about 3.72 million followers (3,722,564), undated snapshot. This is by far the largest Syrian audience of any player found — [Facebook OpenSooq.Syria](https://www.facebook.com/OpenSooq.Syria/); [museum.app](https://museum.app/en/shopping/opensooq-sy)
- Global app (one app for all countries, package com.opensooq.OpenSooq): 50M+ Google Play downloads, rating 4.6 from about 619K reviews. The app claims 60M+ users in 20 countries (snapshot about 201 days old). There is also an iOS app, id654456967 — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.opensooq.OpenSooq); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%81%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%AD-opensooq/id654456967); [APKPure](https://apkpure.com/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%82-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%81%D8%AA%D9%88%D8%AD-opensooq/com.opensooq.OpenSooq)
- Conflicting source: an undated Redseer interview with co-founder Adey Salamin says OpenSooq "lost its presence in Syria and Sudan" because of instability. This contradicts the live Syria site and the Bayt profile that lists Syria among its 20 countries, so the interview is probably older — [Redseer](https://redseer.com/articles/opensooqs-100m-journey-a-conversation-with-adey/); contradicted by [Bayt OpenSooq profile](https://www.bayt.com/en/company/opensooq-1700607/) and [Apify scraper listing sy.opensooq.com](https://apify.com/parsebird/opensooq-scraper)
- Listings show safety tips: meet in a public place, bring someone with you, inspect before paying — [OpenSooq Syria listing](https://sy.opensooq.com/ar/search/281444644)

**Dubizzle / OLX (Dubizzle Group)**
- No current Syria site was found. Around 2015, Dubizzle/OLX listed Egypt, Algeria, Bahrain, KSA, Lebanon, Jordan, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar and Tunisia, without Syria — [Wamda 2015](https://www.wamda.com/2015/09/dubai-classifieds-platform-dubizzle-acquired-by-olx)
- An older (around 2010) article on Dubizzle's early expansion says it launched in Syria, Jordan and the GCC at that time. Syria was dropped later, date unknown — [The National, "five years on dubizzle ready to cast net wider"](https://thenational-the-national-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/business/technology/five-years-on-dubizzle-ready-to-cast-net-wider-1.557802)
- The nearest Levant presence is "dubizzle OLX Lebanon" (iOS id1605794268) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B2%D9%84-%D8%A3%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%83%D8%B3-%D9%84%D8%A8%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%86/id1605794268)

**Facebook Marketplace**
- A third-party tracker page (dated 30 Sep 2026) says Facebook Marketplace is "Blocked" in Syria. It lists local alternatives: Asuaaq, Doushesh, Mnsooq and Damazzle — [UnblockSyria](https://unblocksyria.com/en/services/facebook-marketplace)
- Meta enabled paid/sponsored ads for users in Syria from 2 Oct 2025 (Enab Baladi, 5 Oct 2025). That covers ads, not Marketplace — [Enab Baladi](https://www.enabbaladi.net/777031/%d9%85%d9%8a%d8%aa%d8%a7-%d8%aa%d8%aa%d9%8a%d8%ad-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%a5%d8%b9%d9%84%d8%a7%d9%86%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d9%85%d9%85%d9%88%d9%91%d9%84%d8%a9-%d8%b9%d9%84%d9%89-%d9%85%d9%86%d8%b5/?amp=1)

### Inferences
- The real incumbent is OpenSooq, which has a big Facebook audience and an SYP-priced Syria site. Its Arabic brand "السوق المفتوح" and the derived phrase "سوق سوريا المفتوح" already own the "open souq" idea in Syrian minds.
- Kleinanzeigen/OLX-style global brands are absent, so no international brand name needs to be avoided beyond OpenSooq and Haraj, which both have local clones.

### Gaps
- No Similarweb traffic figures for sy.opensooq.com or any Syrian site could be retrieved (pages not fetchable).
- OpenSooq's logo and colours, and its Syria-specific app downloads, could not be checked. The app is a single global app.
- Official Meta documentation on whether Marketplace is enabled in Syria was not found. Only one third-party tracker says it is blocked.

## Q2. Syria-specific competitors: names, meanings, domains, store presence, positioning, traction

### Takeaway
There is a long tail of about 35 small Syria-only classifieds apps. Only three have notable traction or backing:
- **Doushesh (دوشيش):** 100K+ downloads, pre-seed funding (Jan 2026), plus a planned Saudi Aqar investment of up to $480K (Sep 2026).
- **Damazzle:** founded 2019 and claims to be "number one".
- **OpenSooq:** the regional player from Q1.

Most other apps are generic "Souq/Sooq + Syria", "Haraj Syria", "Mzad Syria" or "Syria Market" names with fewer than 1K downloads.

### Cited Findings

#### A. Funded or most-traction Syria-specific horizontals

**Doushesh — دوشيش (also spelled Dosheesh / Doshish)**
- **Domain:** doushesh.com. Damascus-based, founded 2025 by Alaaeddin Zarzour and Zafer Odabashi.
- **Categories:** real estate, vehicles, electronics, furniture, jobs, services, fashion and equipment.
- **Funding:** closed a pre-seed round in January 2026 for trust and safety, verified listings and expansion across Syria.
- **Sources:** [Wamda Jan 2026 (EN)](https://www.wamda.com/2026/01/syrian-classifieds-platform-doushesh-closes-pre-seed-round); [Wamda (AR)](https://www.wamda.com/2026/01/syrian-classifieds-platform-doushesh-closes-pre-seed-round-arabic); [AIM Group](https://aimgroup.com/2026/01/08/syria-based-classifieds-marketplace-doushesh-closes-pre-seed-funding-round/); [Arageek](https://en.arageek.com/doushesh-secures-pre-seed-funding-to-reinvent-syrias-classifieds-landscape); [doushesh.com](https://doushesh.com/)
- Saudi real estate portal AQAR signed an MoU in Sep 2026 to invest up to $480,000 via a SAFE, released in milestone tranches and still subject to conditions — [Wamda Sep 2026](https://www.wamda.com/2026/09/saudi-aqar-plans-480000-strategic-investment-syria-doushesh); [entarabi](https://entarabi.com/en/2026/09/doushesh-a-syrian-classifieds-platform/); [GCC Startup News](https://www.gccstartup.news/investment/funding-investment/saudi-aqar-plans-up-to-480000/); [Jawlah](https://jawlah.co/en/63744)
- **Traction:**
  - Google Play (package com.doushesh.twa, a "TWA" web-app wrapper) shows 100K+ downloads.
  - AppBrain estimates about 250K downloads.
  - MENA Startup Digest reports a 200,000-download milestone across the iOS and Android stores.
  - On iOS (id6745438438, developer "Zafer Odabasi") it has only 5 ratings, averaging 3.0.
  - Sources: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.doushesh.twa&amp%3Bhl=en_US); [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B4%D9%8A%D8%B4/com.doushesh.twa); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B4%D9%8A%D8%B4/id6745438438); [MENA Startup Digest](https://menastartupdigest.com/?p=29976); [APKCombo](https://apkcombo.com/%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B4%D9%8A%D8%B4/com.doushesh.twa/)
- **Positioning and model:** calls itself "أكبر منصة للإعلانات المبوبة في سوريا" (largest classifieds platform in Syria). It says thousands of new ads are added daily and that free ads are limited in number (freemium). It also claims 100+ categories — [doushesh.com](https://doushesh.com/); [manhom.com](https://manhom.com/%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA/%D9%85%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A9-%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%B4%D9%8A%D8%B4/); [Arab Founders](https://arabfounders.net/en/doushesh-pre-seed-funding-syria/)
- **Name meaning:** no source explains "دوشيش". A doushesh.com category literally named "إلي" suggests playful Levantine copy (unverified).

**Damazzle (Latin only)**
- **Domain:** damazzle.com. Crunchbase lists it as founded 2019 in Damascus, private, with 11–50 employees. Tracxn lists it as "Unfunded" and describes it as a multi-category used-goods marketplace — [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/damazzle); [Tracxn Syria marketplaces](https://tracxn.com/d/explore/marketplaces-startups-in-syria/__kyAYtw7aN0K3gP5jjoAE0YWzILK_zFLAw9c6AgnzDXg/companies)

- **Presence:** Android app com.company.damazzle (free) and Facebook page Damazzle.1. User reviews praise its ease of use and no commission — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.company.damazzle&hl=en); [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/Damazzle.1/)
- **Name:** clearly a Damascus + Dubizzle portmanteau (inference).

## Q5. How Syrians currently trade second-hand goods, and the vocabulary they use

### Takeaway
Facebook groups (and in the north, Telegram channels) are still the default channel, alongside WhatsApp groups and physical flea markets. Groups are typically named "بيع وشراء + [city]" or "بيع وشراء مستعمل + [city]". The everyday vocabulary is بيع وشراء, مستعمل, بالة, سوق الحرامية and سوق الجمعة. Several apps now explicitly market themselves as the "alternative to Facebook groups".

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- The words users already link with trading are بيع وشراء, مستعمل, بالة, حراج, سوق الجمعة and سوق الحرامية, plus the city name. These are useful for app-store ASO keywords and subtitles, but too generic and crowded for the brand name itself.
- "First", "largest", "free", "no commission" and "no middleman" are table stakes in competitor copy. A name or tagline built on trust, verification or local neighbourhood would differ more from the field.
